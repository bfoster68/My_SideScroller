MY SIDE-SCROLLER — Tester Build
================================

GETTING STARTED
---------------
macOS: Double-click "my_sidescroller" to launch.
       If macOS blocks it, right-click > Open > Open anyway.

The "assets" folder MUST stay next to the game executable.


CONTROLS
--------
Movement:    A / D  or  Left / Right arrow keys
Jump:        Space (press again mid-air for double jump)
             Tap for short hop, hold for full jump
Pause:       Escape
Menu:        Up/Down arrows to navigate, Enter/Space to select


OBJECTIVE
---------
Run as far as you can! Jump across platforms, collect coins, stomp
enemies from above, and avoid hazards. Your score increases as you
progress, and the game gets harder the further you go.


ENEMIES
-------
- Walking (red): patrol platforms — stomp from above to kill
- Flying (purple): hover in the air — stompable
- Shooter (green): fire projectiles — stomp or dodge
- Charging (orange): rush at you after a wind-up — jump over them
- Flying ranged: aerial enemies that shoot downward


HAZARDS
-------
- Spikes: static damage on platforms
- Spinning saws: patrol platforms, always hurt (can't stomp)
- Lava: fills ground gaps, instant kill
- Falling boulders: watch for warning markers above
- Timed traps: flash before reactivating


POWER-UPS
---------
- Blue (Speed Boost): +50% speed for 5 seconds
- Green (Triple Jump): 3 jumps instead of 2 for 8 seconds
- Gold (Shield): absorbs one hit


SAVE SYSTEM
-----------
The game auto-saves at checkpoints. You can also manually save:

  Pause > Save Game > choose a slot or create a new one

To load a save:

  Main Menu > Load Game > select a save

To delete a save:

  Load Game screen > highlight a save > press Backspace > Enter to confirm


TIPS
----
- Chain stomps without landing for combo multipliers (up to 16x score!)
- Breakable blocks degrade as you run across them
- Coins are worth more at higher difficulty
- Power-ups spawn more often as difficulty increases
- Every 1000 points you enter a new section


KNOWN ISSUES / FEEDBACK
------------------------
This is a test build. Please note any issues you encounter:
- Platforms that seem unreachable
- Unfair enemy placement
- Performance drops
- Anything that feels wrong or could be improved

Send feedback to the developer. Thanks for testing!
